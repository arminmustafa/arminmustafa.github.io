I am an Associate Professor in Computer Vision and AI at the University of Surrey, where I direct the N4D Lab in CVSSP. My research asks how machines can understand not just what happens in video, but why, by combining 4D vision, which reconstructs people and scenes in 3D as they move, with narrative understanding of video, audio and language.

Over 15 years, working with partners including BBC R&D, Foundry and the CoSTAR National Lab, I have moved this research from the lab into use: audio-visual captioning in BBC pilots, light-field VR film production, and accessible video through SAIReco, the company I co-founded. As an AI Fellow at the Incubator for AI (i.AI), DCMS, I evaluate AI for UK public services. I previously held a five-year Royal Academy of Engineering Research Fellowship in 4D vision for perceptive machines, and my work appears at CVPR, ICCV, NeurIPS and ICLR.

N4D Lab · 4D Narrative AI

AI that understands what happened and why

The N4D Lab studies 4D Narrative AI: combining 4D vision and machine perception with narrative understanding of video. I direct it at CVSSP, University of Surrey.

Research

Four themes, one question

How do you get from pixels and sound to an account of a scene that makes sense to a person?

3D/4D Vision

Can a machine rebuild a moving scene, and everyone in it, from ordinary video?

Spatial AI and 4D machine perception: temporally coherent reconstruction of dynamic scenes from multi-view video and light fields, and of people and objects from a single image.

CVPRICCVECCVIJCV

Narrative Understanding

Can a model explain why one event led to the next, not just what happened?

Captioning that moves from describing what is on screen to explaining why it happened: causal and temporal links between events.

NeurIPSICLRCVPRW

Video Understanding

Can we find every action and sound in long video, cheaply enough to use?

Dense action and event detection in long, untrimmed video, with audio-visual parsing and question answering.

IJCVECCVAAAIWACV

Generative AI

Can we generate people and video that do exactly what we ask?

Controllable generation of people, video and 3D motion from text, pose and style, including text to 3D sign language.

At a glance

Questions people ask

What is 4D Narrative AI?

4D Narrative AI is the research focus of my N4D Lab at the University of Surrey. It combines 4D vision, which reconstructs people and scenes in 3D as they move over time, with narrative understanding of video, so that machines can explain what happened, in what order, and why.

What is video understanding?

Video understanding is about getting machines to make sense of video the way people do: recognising who and what is in a scene, detecting actions and events as they happen, linking what is heard to what is seen, and describing it all in language. My lab works on dense action and event detection, audio-visual parsing and question answering, and captioning that explains cause and effect.

How can I join the lab?

PhD applicants can email me with a CV and a short note on their background and the problem they want to work on. I am happy to hear from candidates interested in 4D vision, video understanding, narrative AI or generative AI.

Can companies and public bodies work with the lab?

Yes. Organisations work with the lab through consultancy, Knowledge Transfer Partnerships, sponsored PhD studentships such as the BBC CASE studentship, and joint research grants. Partners have included BBC R&D, Foundry, Figment Productions, Humain, A*STAR, the Incubator for AI and the Alan Turing Institute. Email me with a short description of the problem.
